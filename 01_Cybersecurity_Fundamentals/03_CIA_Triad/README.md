# CIA Triad Laboratory

## 1. Objective
Understand the three fundamental principles of information security.

## 2. Confidentiality
Protect information from unauthorized access.

Example:
- Strong passwords
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Data encryption

## 3. Integrity
Ensure information remains accurate and is not modified without authorization.

Example:
- Hashing
- Digital signatures
- File integrity monitoring

## 4. Availability
Ensure systems and information are accessible when needed.

Example:
- Backups
- Redundant systems
- Disaster recovery plans

## 5. Practical Scenario
A company experiences a cyberattack.

- Confidentiality: Customer data is exposed.
- Integrity: Financial records are modified.
- Availability: The company website becomes unavailable.

## 6. Conclusion
The CIA Triad helps organizations protect information through confidentiality, integrity and availability.
